Forge

Forge is where you hand work to coding agents and stay in charge without sitting at the laptop.

The agents work on your machines with your subscriptions. Forge keeps the record, runs the checks, and brings you only the decisions that need a person, wherever you are.

Tap a status to light up only those parts on the map.

Four promises

1Nothing goes missing.

Every task, claim, decision and answer is on record.

2Nothing ships unchecked.

Every change has a receipt saying what was checked.

3Nothing waits on you silently.

A stopped agent reaches you, on a ladder you set.

4Nothing happens in your name without you.

Publish, merge and deploy need the real, signed-in you.

The map

Five layers, from you at the top down to the machines that do the work. The band on the side runs through all of them: every part is a block that can be swapped.

The path

Now
  • A terminal tool that tracks work, runs checks and keeps memory, on one laptop.
Next
  • The cloud switches on.
  • Then the question loop: an agent asks, your phone or Slack shows it, your answer goes back.
  • Then the catch-me-up brief and the all-projects home.
Later
  • Teams and handoff.
  • Your own workflows and the flow map.
  • Community blocks and templates.
  • Agents that run with the laptop closed.

What Forge is not

  • It is not an agent.
  • It is not a model.
  • It is not a chat app.
  • It does not store your code.

It does not replace Claude or Codex. It is the layer above them that you stay in charge through.
