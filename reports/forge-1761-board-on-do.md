# Board on a Durable Object

Board's server ran locally inside a Cloudflare Durable Object, using its SQLite storage and alarms. The trial used 75 lines for the storage and alarm adapter, a 59-line Worker, and a 20-line script that makes two patched copies of Board's packaged files. The biggest obstacle was Board's stock backend hardwiring a Node SQLite constructor that failed in Workers and keeping the reusable engine and SQL behind private package paths.

Evidence rung 5 for the running local service. The export and interface findings are backed by the source and by the installed package.

## Conformance skips

All five skips were in the grants group. Board reported zero failed checks.

- "lets everyone narrow what something else lets in, and lets no one in itself" needs a team and project.
- "caps a person by their team role, so a viewer reads but neither posts nor manages" needs a team viewer.
- "names an app by its slug or client ID, keeps it by client ID, and every app as a group" needs an installed app.
- "lets a member grant on a pattern after a lock under it" requires locking the runner out and is left to Board's own tests.
- "lets a project on another team in, in its environments" was skipped because cross-team grants are unspecified.

## Still needed for production

A stable Board extension point or a maintained fork would replace the private imports and keep upgrades testable. Forge would need real owner and agent credentials, explicit access rules, and authorized routing to each board. Production use would also need persistent attachment storage, limits for forwarded request bodies, and tests of alarm retries, concurrent waiters, and hosted latency and cost.
