# Prior art

Before any feature design, post one comment on the intent issue with three parts: Copy, Their mistakes and Decision.

**Copy:** code or designs we reuse, each with source URL and license. MIT, Apache-2.0, and BSD with attribution may be copied. GPL, AGPL, BSL, or no license: ideas only, never code.

**Their mistakes:** known problems from their issue trackers, reverts, changelogs, or user complaints, each quoted with a URL.

**Decision:** what we do and why.

When nothing similar turns up, say "none found" and name where you searched. The research blocks only the decisions that depend on it; continue with the others.
