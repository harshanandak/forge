# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests and other spaces
deserves to take part without harassment. Welcome different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Give specific feedback about the work and receive it
respectfully. Harassment, threats, slurs, personal attacks and sharing someone's
private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Report a concern

Use the [private GitHub form](https://github.com/harshanandak/forge/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct:`. Include the relevant links and share only
what the maintainer needs to investigate. The maintainer handles reports
privately and aims to acknowledge them within 7 days. If the report concerns
the maintainer, use [GitHub's unaffiliated reporting route](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

Depending on the harm and any repeated behavior, the maintainer may remove
content, give a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Retaliation for reporting is also grounds for action.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/).
